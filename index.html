LifeBalance Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026 · App release: 1.4.0

LifeBalance is developed by Green Key Universe. This policy explains how the Android and iOS apps handle information, including optional camera-based exercise counting. Contact: greenkeyuniverse@gmail.com.

1. Information you keep in LifeBalance

Habit names, settings, daily records, exercise results, timers, rewards and reward history are stored in the app’s local storage. Optional reward photos selected by you are also kept locally. LifeBalance does not provide an app account or an application-operated cloud synchronization service. We do not upload your habit database, reward photos or workout history to our own server.

LifeBalance does not ask you to provide your name, email address or precise location to use its core features. If you contact support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send.

2. Camera and exercise recognition

Camera access is optional and is requested for camera-based exercise counting. When you open a camera workout, live frames are processed on your device to estimate body-joint positions and count push-ups, squats, crunches or plank duration. Recognition runs using Google ML Kit on Android and Apple Vision on iOS.

The workout feature does not record a video, store camera frames, or send frames or joint coordinates to Green Key Universe, Google, Amplitude or another remote recognition service. Joint positions are used transiently for on-device analysis, not to identify who you are. A workout result is stored locally when you save it. You can review and correct the result before saving.

On Android, adding a camera template may first offer an additional recognition download through Google Play. Camera permission is requested after recognition is available. The installed recognition module can perform detection without an internet connection. Downloading the module and SDK telemetry are separate from processing the camera frames.

You can deny or revoke camera permission in your device settings. Other habit-tracking features remain available without camera access. This camera permission does not control the separate analytics and diagnostic services described below.

3. Analytics and crash diagnostics

LifeBalance uses Firebase Analytics and Amplitude to understand feature usage and improve the app. Firebase Crashlytics is used on Android to diagnose crashes. SDKs may initialize and send usage or diagnostic information when the app is used; this is not controlled by the camera permission.

Information may include device model and manufacturer, operating system, app version, language, timestamps, screens and button interactions, session information, installation or SDK-generated identifiers, crash stack traces and diagnostic information. Network services may process IP addresses and derive approximate country or city. LifeBalance does not request GPS location for this purpose.

App events can include the selected predefined habit or exercise template, whether a template uses camera tracking, tracking method, premium status and reminder settings. The camera workout implementation does not send frames, body-joint coordinates or the numerical workout result as a workout analytics event. SDK identifiers are pseudonymous, not a guarantee of anonymity. Amplitude is configured to use its EU server zone.

4. ML Kit technical data on Android

On-device recognition does not mean that the SDK never communicates with Google. ML Kit may send technical data such as device and app information, installation identifiers, API configuration, input/output sizes, performance measurements, SDK events and error codes for diagnostics and usage analytics. Pose detection also uses Firebase Installations and Remote Config for SDK configuration and diagnostics. These technical communications do not upload the workout’s images or body-joint coordinates. See ML Kit’s data disclosure and terms and privacy information.

5. Service providers and disclosures

Relevant services include Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, Amplitude, and Google Play and Google ML Kit. Google Play handles Android subscription payments, optional feature delivery and in-app reviews. We do not receive your payment-card details. Apple’s on-device Vision framework handles iOS pose recognition.

We use service providers for app functionality, analytics, diagnostics and responding to support requests. We do not sell user data or use workout images or results for advertising or marketing. Information may also be disclosed when legally required or necessary to protect users and the service. Providers may process information in countries other than yours under their applicable terms and safeguards; Amplitude’s EU setting is not a promise that all services process data only in the EU.

6. Retention and deletion

Local records remain on your device until you delete them through available app controls, clear the app’s storage or remove the app. Camera frames and joint positions are not retained as a recording. Operating-system backups or device transfers, where applicable, are managed separately by your platform and settings; removing the app does not necessarily delete existing backups.

Analytics and diagnostic records are stored separately by the service providers. There is no single fixed retention period for all such data: retention depends on the service, its configured retention settings and the period needed for usage analysis, troubleshooting or applicable legal obligations. Removing the app does not automatically erase information already sent to a provider. Support correspondence is retained as needed to resolve the request and retain necessary support or legal records.

For access, deletion or other privacy requests, contact greenkeyuniverse@gmail.com. We will assess and handle requests under applicable law, including requests involving our providers. Because the app has no user account, an email address alone may not identify an SDK installation; we may ask for limited additional information necessary to locate the records. Do not send workout videos or medical documents for this purpose. Revoking camera access stops further camera use, but does not delete saved records or previously transmitted diagnostics.

7. Security and your choices

We use reasonable measures to protect information, and the listed SDK providers use encrypted network connections for their service communications. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. You control camera and notification permissions in your device settings. You can contact us about your information and applicable privacy rights. An operating-system permission for one feature is not consent to unrelated uses of data.

8. Fitness information

Exercise counts, duration, pace and calorie estimates are intended for personal habit and fitness tracking. Recognition can make mistakes. Calorie values are estimates based on activity duration and a reference body mass of 70 kg, not a measurement of your individual energy expenditure. LifeBalance is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent a medical condition. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

9. Children, external links and this website

We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information that should be removed, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

External websites and app stores have their own privacy practices. This policy website is hosted on GitHub Pages and does not include app analytics, advertising scripts or tracking cookies added by us. GitHub may process visitor IP addresses and technical request data to provide and secure hosting, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app or its data practices change. The updated policy and effective date will be published at this address. Questions and privacy requests: greenkeyuniverse@gmail.com · Green Key Universe.
